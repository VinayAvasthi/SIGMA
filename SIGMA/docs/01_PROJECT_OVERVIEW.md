# Project Overview

## SIGMA

SIGMA is an automated signal-analysis platform for `.IQ` and `.WAV` recordings.

### Objective

Convert raw recordings into structured signal intelligence through a reproducible pipeline:

1. Ingest.
2. Validate.
3. Decode.
4. Preprocess.
5. Detect.
6. Segment.
7. Extract features.
8. Estimate parameters.
9. Classify where applicable.
10. Visualize.
11. Store.
12. Generate reports/API outputs.

### Primary Users

- Signal-analysis teams.
- RF/spectrum-analysis operators.
- Research teams.
- Sensor-processing teams.
- Engineering teams building downstream signal-processing systems.

### Expected Benefit

The proposed workflow reduces repeated manual inspection and standardizes the way measurements and analysis results are produced.
